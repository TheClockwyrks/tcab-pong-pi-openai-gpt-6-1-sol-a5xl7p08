# Carom

Two paddles. One ball. No straight answers. Carom is a quick, top-down duel where a small movement at contact can change the entire rally. Take on the machine in Solo, or sit down with a friend for a local Versus match.

The opening serve gives you a moment to find your feet. Meet the ball in the center of your paddle for a direct return, or catch it near an edge to send it sharply across the field. Keep moving through the hit and your shot takes on spin, bending away from the line your opponent expected.

Two blue bars sway and turn between you. A tilted face can turn a safe return into a surprise attack; a bank off the wall can find a route around an obstacle. Read the moving gaps, follow the fading trail, and make the next angle yours.

Every paddle hit raises the pace. First to eleven wins, but you need a two-point lead: at ten-all, there is no shortcut past the final rally. Stay calm, keep your paddle moving, and find the shot they cannot reach.
